3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

3.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us recognize your browser and remember certain information.

3.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality (e.g., city selection, compare list)
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences
  • Advertising Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements (if applicable)

3.3 Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

3.4 Third-Party Cookies

We may allow third-party services (such as Google Analytics) to place cookies on your device for analytics and advertising purposes. These third parties have their own privacy policies governing their use of information.
