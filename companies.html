FAQs About EV Companies in India

Which is the largest electric vehicle company in India?+
Ola Electric is currently the largest electric two-wheeler company in India by sales volume. In the electric car segment, Tata Motors dominates with over 60% market share.
How many electric vehicle companies are there in India?+
There are over 100 electric vehicle companies operating in India, ranging from established manufacturers like Tata and Mahindra to new-age startups like Ather, Ola Electric, and Ultraviolette.
Which Indian company makes electric cars?+
Major Indian companies making electric cars include Tata Motors (Nexon EV, Tiago EV, Punch EV), Mahindra Electric (XEV 9e, BE 6), and MG Motor India (ZS EV). Hyundai and BYD also have electric car offerings in India.
Which is the best electric scooter company in India?+
Top electric scooter companies in India include Ola Electric, Ather Energy, TVS (iQube), Bajaj (Chetak), VIDA (Hero MotoCorp), and Hero Electric. Each offers different features and price points to suit various needs.
Are electric vehicles cheaper to maintain?+
Yes, electric vehicles are significantly cheaper to maintain than petrol/diesel vehicles. EVs have fewer moving parts, no oil changes, reduced brake wear due to regenerative braking, and lower overall maintenance costs.
